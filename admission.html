Learning

Nurturing curiosity, creativity and confidence since 1959

Our Approach

At Visakha Nursery School, our learning is guided by love, care, and purposeful play. We follow a play-based and activity-based learning approach, where children learn through fun, hands-on experiences that spark curiosity and creativity. Alongside this, we gently nurture Buddhist cultural values such as kindness, respect, mindfulness, and gratitude. We also place great importance on each child’s emotional well-being, helping them feel safe, happy, and confident every day. Our focus is on holistic development—supporting social, emotional, physical, and intellectual growth. Children are also introduced to bilingual or trilingual language exposure, building strong communication and early literacy skills. With love, care, and meaningful learning experiences, we help each child grow into a confident, kind, and joyful learner.

Language Development

At Visakha Nursery, we support children's language growth using both English and Sinhala instructions. Through stories, songs, daily conversations, and fun games, children learn to understand and speak confidently in both languages. We create a playful and caring environment to make language learning natural and enjoyable.

Religious and Cultural Values

At Visakha Nursery, our foundation is rooted in Buddhist cultural values, nurturing kindness, mindfulness, and compassion in young children. While we proudly uphold these traditions, we also embrace and respect all religions and cultures. Children are taught to appreciate diversity, practice tolerance, and value harmony in a multicultural society. Our aim is to create an inclusive environment where children grow up with strong moral values, mutual respect, and understanding, preparing them to become respectful and responsible citizens.

Elocution and English language

At Visakha Nursery School, we offer Elocution classes under the CALSDA (Colombo Academy of Language Skills) program. This helps children improve their spoken English, pronunciation, expression, and confidence. Through poems, speech exercises, and role play, children develop excellent communication and performance skills in a fun and creative way.

Music

Music plays a joyful role in our daily routine. Children sing songs, play simple instruments, and move to rhythms, helping them develop listening skills, coordination, and creativity. Music time brings happiness while supporting emotional and language development.

Dancing

Dancing helps our children express themselves with joy and confidence. Through traditional and modern movements, they learn rhythm, coordination, and teamwork. Dance sessions also support physical development and build stage confidence for performances.

Art and Craft

Our art and craft sessions spark creativity and imagination. Children explore colors, shapes, and textures while developing fine motor skills. From painting to paper crafts, each activity encourages self-expression and joy in creating something special.

Computing

Our young learners are introduced to basic computing skills in a fun and safe way. Through age-appropriate educational games and activities, children develop early tech awareness, hand-eye coordination, and problem-solving skills to prepare for the digital world.

Tactile Material

We use tactile materials like sandpaper letters, textured shapes, beads, and puzzles to support hands-on learning. These materials help children develop fine motor skills, sensory awareness, and early literacy and math concepts through touch and play.

Maths Concepts

We introduce basic maths concepts through play and hands-on activities. Children learn about numbers, shapes, sizes, patterns, and counting using toys, blocks, puzzles, and songs. This builds a strong foundation for problem-solving and logical thinking.

STEAM Learning

We introduce STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Maths) through fun, hands-on activities. Children explore, experiment, build, and create—developing curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking. STEAM learning lays the foundation for problem-solving and innovation from a young age.

Play

Play is at the heart of learning at Visakha Nursery. Through indoor and outdoor play, children develop social skills, creativity, coordination, and confidence. We believe play is the best way for young minds to explore, imagine, and grow happily.

Other Facilities

Library

Our library is a cozy, child-friendly space filled with colorful storybooks and educational material. It encourages early reading habits, imagination, and language development in a quiet and nurturing environment.

Sick Room

The sick room is available for children who feel unwell during school hours. It is equipped with a comfortable bed and basic first-aid facilities to ensure the child rests safely until parents are contacted.

Playground

The spacious outdoor playground is designed with child-safe play equipment. It provides children with the opportunity to play, exercise, and develop motor skills while enjoying the fresh air and sunshine.

Day Care Facility

Our daycare service is available for working parents who need a safe, caring, and structured environment for their children after school hours. Children are supervised by trained caregivers and engaged in fun and educational activities.

Activity Room

The activity room is a vibrant and interactive space where children engage in subject-related, play-based learning. It includes special corners such as:

  • Nature Corner: Encourages observation and curiosity through natural items like leaves, plants, stones, and seeds.
  • Sand Corner: Develops sensory and fine motor skills through creative play with sand and tools.
  • Imagination Corner: A space where children role-play and express their creativity, enhancing social and emotional development.

After School Classes

To keep children actively engaged and support their overall development, we offer a variety of after-school classes, including:

  • Music (Vocal & Instrumental): Enhances rhythm, listening, and musical expression.
  • Dancing: Builds coordination, confidence, and cultural appreciation through expressive movement, rhythm, and group interaction, helping children develop physical awareness and emotional expression.
  • Art and Craft: Sparks creativity & improves fine motor skills through hands-on activities.
  • Elocution: Develops clear speech, confidence, & effective communication skills.

Visakha Nursery School is dedicated to fostering a nurturing and inspiring environment where young minds flourish through curiosity, care, and creativity.
