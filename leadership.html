Leadership

Nurturing curiosity, creativity and confidence since 1959

“ It is with great pride and a deep sense of responsibility that I lead Visakha Nursery School - a nurturing space where the foundation of lifelong learning is lovingly laid. I believe that every child is unique, and it is my mission to guide and support each one as they begin their journey of discovery and growth. At Visakha Nursery, I strive to create an environment that is safe, joyful, and inspiring. I take great care in planning learning experiences that spark curiosity, build confidence, and encourage creativity. I value the individuality of every child and do my best to help them grow into kind, independent, and resilient individuals. I am deeply committed to ensuring that each child feels loved, respected, and empowered. Their happiness and wellbeing are at the heart of everything I do. As I look ahead, I remain determined to prepare my little Visakhians not only for school, but for life - with a strong foundation in values, a love for learning, and the courage to explore the world around them. I warmly invite all parents and well-wishers to walk this journey with me, as I continue to shape the future with care, dedication, and hope. ”

— Mrs. Thushani Gunasekara
Head of Visakha Nursery School
