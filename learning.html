Toddler
Group 🌼

Age group : 2 to 2½ years

🌼At Visakha Nursery, our Toddler Group offers a warm, nurturing environment where the youngest learners begin their preschool journey. This stage focuses on emotional security, basic social interaction, and early communication. Through guided play, music, rhymes, and sensory activities, toddlers begin to explore the world around them. Our caring teachers help each child adapt to the classroom setting at their own pace, fostering independence and a love for learning from the start.

Lower
Nursery 🌿

Age group: 2½ to 3 years

🌿In the Lower Nursery, children begin to engage in more structured activities while continuing to enjoy plenty of play-based learning. At Visakha Nursery, this stage emphasizes language development, early motor skills, and socialization. Children are encouraged to express themselves, follow simple instructions, and build confidence through hands-on learning experiences, art, storytelling, and outdoor play. Gentle routines help them feel secure and develop positive behavior patterns.

Middle
Nursery 🌸

Age group: 3 to 4 years

🌸 Our Middle Nursery group focuses on preparing children for more advanced learning by strengthening pre-literacy, numeracy, and fine motor skills. At this stage, children participate in group work, creative expression, role play, and educational games. Teachers at Visakha Nursery guide them through meaningful activities that spark curiosity, develop communication, and enhance problem-solving abilities. Emotional and social development remains a key focus as children learn to share, take turns, and cooperate.

Upper
Nursery 🌻

Age group: 4 to 5 years

🌻 The Upper Nursery stage at Visakha Nursery is designed to prepare children for a smooth transition to formal schooling. Emphasis is placed on developing reading readiness, early writing skills, basic math concepts, and critical thinking. Children engage in theme-based projects, show-and-tell, drama, music, and gross motor development through outdoor activities. Independence, responsibility, and good manners are encouraged as children become confident and school-ready individuals.
