About Us

Nurturing curiosity, creativity and confidence since 1959

The Beginning of a Vision Rooted in Compassion: The Legacy of Mrs. Jeremias Dias

In an era when the education of girls was an afterthought in many parts of the world, a remarkable woman in colonial Ceylon rose above societal norms and personal loss to plant the seeds of a revolution. Mrs. Jeremias Dias, born Selestina Rodrigo in 1858, belonged to the affluent Rodrigo family of Panadura. Though her own schooling ended after the fifth standard, her spirit of inquiry, business acumen, and devotion to Buddhist values led her to become one of the most influential female philanthropists of her time. Widowed in 1902, she inherited her husband’s vast business empire and managed it with great wisdom and strength — a feat almost unheard of for women in that era.

Building a Legacy in Education

But it was not wealth that defined her legacy; it was her unwavering commitment to empowering girls through education. In 1917, she laid the foundation for what would become Visakha Vidyalaya — a place dedicated to nurturing confident, educated, and value-driven young women. Her legacy of compassion and foresight continued to inspire generations. In the spirit of her vision, Visakha Nursery School was later established — a haven where the seeds of curiosity, kindness, and lifelong learning could be gently sown during the earliest years of a child's life.

Mrs. Jeremias Dias — A visionary in girls’ education

The Legacy Continues: Birth of Visakha Nursery School

On March 2, 1959, the next chapter in her legacy began — the birth of Visakha Nursery School. Built upon the vision that education should begin at the roots, this institution welcomed 150 young learners in its first year. Founded with the same spirit of compassion, cultural pride, and Buddhist values, the nursery school offered not just preschooling, but a life-shaping foundation. Through joyful learning, mindfulness, and creative exploration, it became a sanctuary where children were nurtured to blossom as confident, caring individuals — just as Mrs. Dias would have wished.

Today’s Vision: Preserved by the Board of Trustees

Visakha Nursery School stands as a living tribute to a legacy rooted in wisdom, compassion, and purpose. Though not state-governed, it thrives under a dedicated Board of Trustees — a structure ensuring that every decision centers on the child’s best interest. The school remains grounded in the timeless values of its visionary founder, Mrs. Jeremias Dias: integrity, kindness, wisdom, and faith. Her noble vision has been lovingly carried forward across generations. It was first guided by her grandson, Mr. Nanda Dias, who strengthened its foundation, then by Dr. Tissa Cooray, whose leadership brought new depth. Today, that same spirit lives on through his daughter, Mrs. Ayomi Kelegama, who, alongside the Board, continues to nurture young minds. As a charitable institution, Visakha Nursery School remains a place where tradition and heart-centered learning thrive — true to the legacy from which it was born.

A Future Rooted in the Past

Through bilingual instruction, hands-on learning, and daily rituals of gratitude and mindfulness, the school inspires every child to flourish in both heart and mind. It is not just a nursery — it is a sacred garden where young minds begin to blossom. In every curious question, every gentle smile, and every song sung under the morning sky, lives the legacy of a woman who dared to dream for all of Sri Lanka’s children. Mrs. Jeremias Dias may have passed on in 1933, but her spirit still walks the corridors, shaping every generation with love and light.

FAQ

1 When and how was Visakha Nursery School started?

Visakha Nursery School was established on March 2, 1959 with 150 children, thanks to the generosity of philanthropist Mrs. Jeramias Dias. Her legacy continues today under the guidance of a dedicated Board of Trustees.

2 Who manages the school now?

The school is governed by the Board of Trustees, who provide overall strategic direction. The day-to-day operations are managed by the current leadership team, working in close collaboration with the board to ensure the school’s vision and values are upheld.

3 What Makes Visakha Nursery School Unique?

A 66-year heritage, structured leadership, strong Buddhist values, bilingual instruction, and a creative, holistic curriculum define our approach to early childhood education.

4 What is the religious foundation of the school?

We are a Buddhist school. Each day begins with a religious observance, and we hold a Pre-Poya program before every full-moon Poya day, nurturing respect, discipline, and mindfulness in children.

5 In what languages are children taught?

Children learn in both Sinhala and English, supporting national identity and international readiness by building bilingual confidence.

6 What learning methods and materials do you use?

We follow a child-centered, play-based approach with tactile materials that build independence, concentration, and hands-on curiosity.

7 What creative subjects are offered at Visakha Nursery School?

Music, dance, art, craft, drama, and storytelling nurture imagination, self-expression, and emotional growth from the earliest years.

8 Are there facilities for physical activity?

Yes. Our playground offers age-appropriate equipment where children build gross-motor skills, social cooperation, and pure joy through outdoor play.

9 Why should parents choose Visakha Nursery School?

Because we unite an illustrious history, experienced staff, balanced learning, and compassionate values—helping children flourish mentally, emotionally, socially, and spiritually in a safe, joyful space.
