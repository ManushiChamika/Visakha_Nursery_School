About Our School

Visakha Nursery School, rooted in a proud legacy of excellence, provides a nurturing and engaging environment for early childhood education. Located in Colombo 04, it focuses on fostering curiosity, creativity, and kindness in young learners. Welcoming both girls and boys, the nursery lays a strong foundation for academic and personal growth in each child’s learning journey.
